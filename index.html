🎉 The Ungame – Fun Rules! 🎉

Easy, silly, and friendly for all ages.

🤔 How Do We Play?

1. Pick How Long You Want to Play ⏱️

  • Most people play for about 45 minutes.
  • But you're the boss, play as long as you want!

2. Sit Together Like a Cozy Crew 🧸

  • Everyone sits close so you can hear each other.
  • 2 to 8 players is perfect!

3. No Talking Over Each Other 🤐

  • When it's your turn, you get to talk!
  • Everyone else listens like a superhero listener. 🦸‍♂️👂

4. Choose Your Deck! 🎴

  • Deck #1: Fun, silly, light topics.
  • Deck #2: More serious stuff. Save this for later!

5. Draw a Card... GO! 🎉

  • If it's a TOPIC card, read it out loud and share your answer in 2–3 sentences.
  • No one else can comment yet!
  • If it's a QUESTION/COMMENT card, you can ask ONE person a question or make a comment.

6. Pass the Turn to the Left 🔄

  • Keep going until time’s up!
  • Chat more afterward if you want.

✨ Why It’s Awesome!

  • Everyone gets a turn to talk 🎤
  • No interrupting 🚫🙊
  • You learn cool things about each other 🤯
  • It teaches listening like a pro 🧠👂
  • No winning or losing — just fun! 🥳

👨‍👩‍👧 Who Can Play?

  • Families
  • Friends
  • Kids at school
  • Church groups
  • Counselors & teachers
  • Birthday parties 🎂
  • Anyone who likes to talk and listen!
